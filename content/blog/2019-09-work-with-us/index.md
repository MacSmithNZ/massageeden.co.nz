Massage Me Day Spa has been operating in Auckland Central for over 6 years. Now we are looking to expand our business whilst still focusing on offering a valueable, and high-end service. Helping our clients improve their health and general well-being. Our attention to detail and attentiveness to our clients needs is what has propelled us forward as one of Auckland’s fastest growing day spa venues.

We are looking for experienced therapists to bring in their unique skillset and passion for excellence. With expertise in both Eastern and Western massage techniques, and the ability to provide beauty treatment. Someone who is at home in a luxury day spa environment.
The Potential Candidates should have these qualities:

- At lease 3 years experience in the spa industry at a five star hotel.

- Certification in Eastern massage techniques; including facials and body treatments (CIBTAC or CIDESCO will be an advantage).

- The ability to interact with guests in a professional manner and exceptional service skills.

- Good command of the English language. Any additional languages will be an advantage.

- We require therapists to have a high level of ethics and posses outstanding guest service skills with experience.

We expect some prior knowledge of the following Eastern and Western techniques.
Eastern massage styles include:

- Balinese massage
- Thai massage
- Shiatsu Massage
- Reflexology
- Herbal Hot compress
- Eastern head massage

Western massage styles include:

- Aromatherapy massage
- Deep tissue massage
- Swedish massage
- Hot stone massage
- Pregnancy massage
- Lomi lomi Massage

As well as Facial Treatments, Body Treatments and waxing with certification.

If you would like to be part of our team send your CV and cover letter to [jenny@massagemedayspa.co.nz](mailto:jenny@massagemedayspa.co.nz)

If your personality and work experience matches the description above and you would like to join our team please don’t hesitate to contact us.

Applicants for this position should have NZ residency or a valid NZ work visa.
