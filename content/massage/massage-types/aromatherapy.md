## What is Aromatherapy?

Now a lot of people may not know what aromatherapy is or the benefits for your health it entails. Aromatherapy is a holistic healing treatment that utilizes natural plant extracts to improve your health and well-being. Also known as ‘Oil Therapy’. Aromatherapy uses aromatic essential oils medicinally to improve help improve the health of your body, mind and spirit. It boosts both emotional and physical health.

We at Massage Eden like to think of aromatherapy as both an art and a science and recently, aromatherapy has gained recognition in the fields of medicine and science.

## How Does Aromatherapy Treatment Work?

Aromatherapy works via your sense of smell and skin absorption using products such as:

- Clay masks
- Hot & cold compresses
- Facial steamers
- Body oils, creams, or lotions for massage application
- Bathing salts
- Inhalers
- Aromatic spritzers
- Diffusers
Using any of these alone or in combination can count as aromatherapy.

## Aromatherapy Benefits

Aromatherapy has a large range of benefits. It is said to help:

- Boost immunity
- Improve hospice and palliative care
- Improve digestion
- Fight bacteria, virus, or fungus
- Ease discomforts of labour
- Alleviate the side effects of chemotherapy
- Treat headaches and migraines
- Soothe sore joints
- Reduce stress, agitation, and anxiety
- Improve sleep quality
- Manage pain

- Lavender
- Lemon
- Lemongrass
- Mandarin
- Neroli
- Patchouli
- Peppermint
- Roman chamomile
- Rose
- Rosemary
- Tea tree
- Vetiver

You can utilize these oils in any number of ways. Such as, adding them to body lotions or carrier oils, then applying them topically. Try boosting a facial toner, conditioner or shampoo with essential oils. They can be incorporated with liquid soap, toothpaste, or mouthwash. You could try diffusing or spritzing the oils throughout a room. Or pour them into a bath.
