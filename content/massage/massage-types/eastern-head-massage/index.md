#### What is Eastern Head Massage?

Eastern Head Massage, also known as Indian Head Massage or Champissage, has been practiced for centuries in India as part of Ayurvedic medicine. This holistic therapy focuses on massaging the head, neck, shoulders, and upper arms to balance the body's energy and release tension.
