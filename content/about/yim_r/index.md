### When did you start your journey with massage?

'I started practising massage in Thailand at Chiva-Sam International Academy. After graduating, I went to Australia to train and gain more experience. While there, I found a job and worked there for 2 years. After those two years, I came to New Zealand in November 2014.'
 
### What first interested you about massage?
 
'I like massage, I like to be massaged, I like the feeling of well-being it provides. When you get a massage, you feel relaxed and we forget the problems of the outside world for a while.'
 
### What is your favourite massage technique?
 
'I don't really have a favourite type of massage. It all depends on what my body needs at that moment. but most of the time I find myself enjoying deep tissue or a relaxing massage.'
 
### What do you like about working at Massage Me Day Spa?
 
'I appreciate the fact that is easy-going and flexible. If one of our staff has a problem, there is always someone to help. I enjoy the friendly atmosphere.'
 
### What massage technique do you most recommend to your customers?
 
'I like Fusion Massage the best because it is a combination of many techniques (Eastern, Western and local techniques). Focusing on treating tension or painful areas whilst helping you relax and relieve stress.'
 
### What massage training have you received?

'I have received training in:
 
- Deep tissue,
- Swedish,
- Aromatherapy,
- Balinese,
- Therapeutic Thai,
- Hot stone,
- Aromatherapy,
- Pressure point, 
- Herbal hot compress,
- Facial treatment'
