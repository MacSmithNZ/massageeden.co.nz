To make your visit as convenient as possible, here’s what you need to know about parking at each of our locations:

### Auckland City

For those visiting our Auckland City location, parking options are available nearby. If you plan to do some shopping after your massage, you can park at Woolworths and enjoy 90 minutes of free parking. Alternatively, paid parking spots are available near the Scene One building, located just across the road from our location.

### Auckland Parnell

At our Parnell location, we’re pleased to offer free parking right behind the building. Simply ask us for a parking card before you drive in, and you’ll be set to enjoy your time with us without any parking worries.

### Rotorua

In Rotorua, paid parking is conveniently located around the corner from our facility. However, if you’re visiting us on a Sunday or a public holiday, you’ll be glad to know that parking is free on those days.
